# Computer Science — List of Bachelor's Exam Topics

From the academic year 2025/2026.

Список тем для подготовки к защите диплома. Сохранён из сообщения пользователя 8 октября 2026 года. Ниже — исходные формулировки на английском языке; переносы строк объединены.

1. Real number sequences. Convergence of a sequence, Cauchy’s principle.
2. Matrices. Basic matrix operations. Rank and determinant of a matrix.
3. Solving systems of linear equations.
4. Propositional calculus. Tautologies
5. Mathematical induction.
6. Permutations, variations and combinations.
7. Classical definition of probability. Geometric probability.
8. Number representations in a positional number system. Binary and hexadecimal systems and their applications.
9. Fixed-point and floating-point arithmetic. Representing numbers in a computer.
10. Operating system. Perception of the operating system by the application software layer.
11. Characteristics of a traditional Unix system
12. Iteration, versus recursion.
13. Comparison of structured and object-oriented programming.
14. Simple and complex types. Pointer type.
15. Data encapsulation
16. Basic features of object-oriented programming: abstraction, inheritance and polymorphism.
17. Structured programming mechanisms - conditional statements, loops.
18. Subroutines. Parameter passing.
19. Vector vs Raster graphics
20. Graphic file formats.
21. Color models in computer graphics
22. Elementary and non-elementary sorting methods.
23. Linked lists and trees - implementations and applications.
24. Stacks and queues - possible implementations and applications.
25. The definition of a graph. Methods of searching graphs.
26. Search methods (linear, binary, Hash-based search.).
27. Computational complexity of an algorithm.
28. Notion of a database - features and capabilities.
29. Relation and its attributes in a database.
30. Referential integrity in relational databases.
31. Database normalization – normal forms.
32. Database design – relationship types, primary and foreign keys.
33. A database index – types and applications.
34. Basic SQL language constructions.
35. Layers and functions of the ISO OSI model.
36. Logical addressing in computer networks.
37. The TCP/IP Protocol Family.
38. Software life cycles.
39. The process of testing and its role in software development.
40. UML, its structure and purpose.
41. Basic project team roles and responsibilities
42. Basic principles of operation of artificial intelligence algorithms and their applications.
43. The structure and operation of artificial neural networks
44. Theoretical models of computers: finite automata, pushdown automata, Turing machines and the corresponding classes of formal languages and grammars
